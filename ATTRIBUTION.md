## phuryn/pm-skills

**Source:** https://github.com/phuryn/pm-skills
**Copyright (c) 2026 Pawel Huryn**

**What we adapted:** The product-management skill bodies and frameworks — discovery (ideation, assumptions, opportunity-solution trees, interviews), strategy (canvases, positioning, pricing, red-teaming), execution (PRDs, user stories, OKRs, roadmaps, release notes), market research (personas, segmentation, journey maps, sentiment, competitive analysis), and go-to-market (beachhead, ICP, battlecards, North Star). Each was re-grounded in the Evermuse MCP so its outputs are backed by real customer evidence.

## github/spec-kit

**Source:** https://github.com/github/spec-kit
**Copyright GitHub, Inc.**

**What we adapted:** The Spec-Driven Development templates and methodology — the feature-spec structure (prioritized, independently-testable user stories; Given/When/Then acceptance scenarios; functional requirements; measurable, technology-agnostic success criteria; `[NEEDS CLARIFICATION]` markers), the implementation-plan and tasks templates (phased, MVP-first, dependency-ordered), and the `analyze` and `clarify` workflows (cross-artifact consistency checks; structured, high-impact clarification questions). These shape the `write-feature-spec`, `development-plan`, and `gap-analysis` skills.
