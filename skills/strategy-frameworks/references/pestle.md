# PESTLE Analysis

Macro-environment scan: **Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental**. Complements SWOT (macro vs. micro). Use early in strategy to avoid blind spots.

## Grounding rule
PESTLE is mostly market-facing → fill each factor from `context` searches (market/regulatory/technology shifts, macro news), each cited. Where a macro shift shows up in customer language (e.g. budget pressure in an economic downturn, a compliance ask), pull that from `evidence` and quote it — it makes the factor concrete and current rather than a textbook generality.

## Categories (3–5 factors each, only what you can cite)
- **Political:** policies, incentives, tariffs, stability, trade rules, licensing.
- **Economic:** growth/GDP, rates, inflation, FX, spending/confidence, labor costs, access to capital.
- **Social:** demographics, cultural attitudes, lifestyle/behavior shifts, digital adoption.
- **Technological:** emerging tech (AI, etc.), digital transformation, security/privacy, automation, adoption rates.
- **Legal:** data-protection (GDPR/CCPA), employment, IP, consumer protection, industry-specific rules, compliance cost.
- **Environmental:** climate/ESG regulation, emissions, resource scarcity, sustainability expectations.

## Output process
Per factor: impact (H/M/L) × probability (H/M/L), cited → prioritize by impact×probability → strategic response (leverage / mitigate / comply) → leading indicators to monitor → contingency plans for high-impact factors → note unknowns needing research.
