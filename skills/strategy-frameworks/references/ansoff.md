# Ansoff Matrix

Growth options across two axes — **Products** (current/new) × **Markets** (current/new) — yielding four quadrants of increasing risk.

|  | Current Market | New Market |
|---|---|---|
| **Current Product** | Market Penetration (low risk) | Market Development (medium) |
| **New Product** | Product Development (medium) | Diversification (high) |

## Grounding rule
Each quadrant's opportunities must trace to a result, not brainstorming:
- **Market Penetration & Product Development** (current market) → ground in `evidence` — expansion use-cases, unmet needs, and adjacent asks customers actually voiced; quote them.
- **Market Development & Diversification** (new markets/products) → ground in `context` — where the market is moving, adjacent segments, competitor whitespace; cited. Corroborate demand with `evidence` where any exists.

## Quadrants (2–3 cited opportunities each)
1. **Market Penetration** — more usage, new use-cases in base, win competitors' customers, reduce churn, upsell. Risk: low. ~6–12 mo.
2. **Market Development** — new geographies/segments/channels, localization. Risk: medium. ~12–24 mo.
3. **Product Development** — new features/adjacent lines/bundles for existing customers. Risk: medium. ~12–18 mo.
4. **Diversification** — new products in new markets (related or unrelated). Risk: high. 24+ mo.

## Output process
Per quadrant: 2–3 cited opportunities, market size, resources, competitive dynamics, success metric → prioritize by strategic fit × revenue potential × feasibility × defensibility → GTM for top 2–3 → phased roadmap → risks + mitigations. Excel in one quadrant before spreading; sequence penetration → development.
