# SWOT Analysis

Internal→external assessment: **Strengths** and **Weaknesses** (internal) × **Opportunities** and **Threats** (external). SWOT is micro; pair with PESTLE for macro.

## Grounding rule
- **Strengths / Weaknesses** are the customer-derived cells → fill from `evidence` (what customers praise vs. complain about), each with a verbatim quote and source badge. A weakness with no customer complaint behind it is filler — cut it.
- **Opportunities / Threats** are the market cells → fill from `context` (competitor moves, market/regulatory shifts, emerging alternatives), each cited.

## Cells (aim for 5–7 each, only what you can cite)
- **Strengths (internal, +):** capabilities, brand, loyalty, IP, cost/efficiency, team, distribution — grounded in what customers value.
- **Weaknesses (internal, −):** resource gaps, tech limits, low awareness, churn drivers, cost structure, key-person dependence — grounded in customer frustration.
- **Opportunities (external, +):** growing segments, tech advances, regulatory tailwinds, competitor gaps, adjacent markets — grounded in context.
- **Threats (external, −):** stronger competitors, shifting preferences, disruption, regulation, downturn — grounded in context.

## Output process
Populate cells (cited) → cross-reference: leverage Strengths×Opportunities (Build), fix Weaknesses to blunt Threats (Defend), Opportunities that overcome Weaknesses (Pivot), Threats that exploit Weaknesses (watch/Exit) → 3–5 recommendations with owners and metrics, each traceable to its cells.
