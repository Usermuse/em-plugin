# Lean Canvas (alternate template)

Fast, hypothesis-driven adaptation of the Business Model Canvas (Ash Maurya). Replaces Partners/Activities/Resources with Problem/Solution/Unfair Advantage. Best for rapid hypothesis testing on a new venture, not as a finished strategy document.

## Grounding note
Same Evermuse loop as `../SKILL.md`. The **Problem** and **Customer Segments** boxes are the evidence-backed heart of this canvas — fill them from `evidence` searches and a quote-angled `search`, cited. **Channels** and **Unfair Advantage** framing lean on `context`. Save `nature: "guidance"`, tags `["evermuse-plugin","business-model","lean-canvas"]`.

## Template

**Product definition**
1. **Problem** — top 3 customer problems + current unsatisfactory solutions. **Quote them verbatim from evidence, cited.**
2. **Solution** — top 3 features/approaches, each mapped to a problem.
3. **Unique Value Proposition (UVP)** — one memorable line; why customers choose you.
4. **Unfair Advantage** — what can't be easily copied (network effects, brand, IP, switching costs). Ground competitor claims in `context`.

**Market & traction**
5. **Customer Segments** — target customer + early adopters. **Grounded in cited evidence.**
6. **Channels** — acquisition/distribution paths (context-backed).
7. **Revenue Streams** — pricing model, LTV, growth assumptions.

**Economics & validation**
8. **Cost Structure** — fixed + variable costs, CAC.
9. **Key Metrics** — activation, retention, revenue, North Star.

## Output process
Define the problem(s) from evidence → 2–3 solutions → UVP → unfair advantage → 1–2 segments → channels → revenue → costs → 3–5 metrics. Surface the riskiest assumptions first and pair each with a cheap validation experiment (landing page, interviews, MVP).

## Known limits (why you may prefer Startup Canvas)
Lean Canvas mixes strategy and business model: "Problem" overlaps Market Segments and "Solution" overlaps Value Proposition; it lacks vision, trade-offs, relative costs, and a coherence check. For strategic clarity *plus* a business model, use `startup-canvas.md`.
