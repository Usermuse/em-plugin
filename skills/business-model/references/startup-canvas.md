# Startup Canvas (alternate template)

Separates strategy from business model (Paweł Huryn): the 9-section Product Strategy Canvas **plus** Cost Structure & Revenue Streams. Recommended for new products that need both strategic clarity and a business model — unlike BMC and Lean Canvas, which blend the two.

## Grounding note
Same Evermuse loop as `../SKILL.md`. Run `guidance` first (existing strategy/objectives/values), then `evidence` for segment problems and desired outcomes, then `context` for costs/channels/competition. Segment problems and value props are **evidence-backed and cited**; competition/channels lean on `context`. Save `nature: "guidance"`, tags `["evermuse-plugin","business-model","startup-canvas"]`.

## Template

### Part 1 — Product Strategy (9 sections)
1. **Vision** — aspiration + values; anchor in customer-desired-future evidence.
2. **Market Segments** — defined by problems (JTBD, desired outcomes, constraints). **First segment + why first, grounded in a cited pain.**
3. **Relative Costs** — low cost vs. unique value.
4. **Value Proposition** (per segment) — What before (pain, quoted [evidence]) → How → What after (outcome) → Alternatives.
5. **Trade-offs** — what you will NOT do.
6. **Key Metrics** — North Star + OMTM this quarter.
7. **Growth** — PLG vs. SLG; channels.
8. **Capabilities** — build vs. partner.
9. **Can't/Won't** — why the integrated strategy is hard to copy.

### Part 2 — Business Model
10. **Cost Structure** — recurring vs. one-off; how costs scale.
11. **Revenue Streams** — pricing approach (penetration, value-based, usage-based, SaaS); scalability + biggest uncertainties.

## Output process
Vision → 2–3 segments (JTBD, cited pains) → cost positioning → value props → trade-offs → metrics → growth → capabilities → defensibility → cost structure → revenue streams → coherence check (do all elements reinforce each other?) → hypotheses + cheapest tests.
